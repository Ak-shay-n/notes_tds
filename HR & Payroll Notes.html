HR & Payroll Notes

Quick fundamentals for an aspiring HR executive (HR operations). Focus: salary TDS & payroll compliance (India).

8 core HR functions

1. Recruitment & Selection

  • Post vacancies, source candidates
  • Screen resumes, run interviews
  • Select the right people for the right roles

2. Onboarding

  • Joining formalities and documents
  • Explain policies, rules, culture
  • Coordinate induction & training

3. Employee Relations

  • Resolve conflicts with management
  • Handle grievances
  • Build teamwork and a positive workplace

4. Payroll & Benefits ⭐

  • Salaries, bonuses, incentives
  • Leave, attendance, insurance, benefits
  • Make sure payroll follows the law

5. Training & Development

  • Find skill gaps
  • Organise training sessions
  • Support career growth

6. Performance Management

  • Appraisals and feedback
  • Help managers set goals
  • Promotions & improvement plans (PIPs)

7. Policies & Compliance ⭐

  • Ensure policies are followed
  • Keep records confidential
  • Labour law and workplace safety compliance

8. Engagement & Retention

  • Team-building and engagement programs
  • Recognise contributions
  • Raise satisfaction, cut turnover

Monthly payroll cycle

  1. Collect inputs: attendance, leaves, new joiners, exits, overtime, incentives, loans
  2. Compute gross: pro-rate for joiners/leavers and LOP days
  3. Apply deductions: EPF, ESI, PT, TDS, other recoveries
  4. Review & approve: compare with last month, check variances and errors
  5. Pay salary: bank transfer / bulk upload
  6. Issue payslips to employees
  7. Pay statutory dues (TDS, PF, ESI, PT) by due dates and file returns
  8. Keep records: payroll registers, challans, proofs
Golden rule: payroll mistakes hit people’s money. Always cross-check inputs (attendance and joining/exit dates cause most errors).

Other pay-related laws (know the names)

  • Payment of Bonus Act: statutory bonus, roughly 8.33% to 20% of wages for eligible employees
  • Payment of Gratuity Act: after 5 years of service; formula = last basic × 15/26 × years of service
  • Minimum Wages: set by state/central government, varies by skill and industry
  • Labour Codes: the four new codes (Wages, Social Security, Industrial Relations, OSH) are replacing the older separate Acts. Under the Code on Wages, basic-type wages are expected to form a large share (around 50%) of CTC, which affects PF and gratuity. Check the current status.

TDS on salary (the big one)

TDS = Tax Deducted at Source. The employer estimates each employee’s total tax for the year, deducts it from salary every month, and deposits it with the government. Under the old Income-tax Act this is Section 192. The new Income Tax Act, 2025 (in force from 1 Apr 2026) renumbers sections, so it appears as Section 392. The concept is the same; always check the current section and form names on incometax.gov.in.

How monthly TDS is calculated

  1. Estimate annual taxable salary (gross for the year, incl. expected bonus)
  2. Subtract the standard deduction and allowed exemptions/deductions
  3. Apply the slab rates of the chosen regime
  4. Apply rebate (Sec 87A) if eligible, then add 4% cess
  5. Divide by 12 (adjusted for tax already deducted and months left)

Other payroll statutory items

EPF (Provident Fund)

  • Applies to establishments with 20+ employees
  • Employee 12% + employer 12% of PF wages (basic + DA)
  • Employer’s 12% splits: 8.33% to EPS (pension, on wages capped at ₹15,000) and 3.67% to EPF
  • Extra employer charges: EDLI and admin charges
  • Each employee has a UAN
  • Pay via ECR on the EPFO portal by the 15th of next month

ESI (Insurance)

  • For employees earning gross up to ₹21,000/month
  • Employee 0.75% + employer 3.25% of gross wages
  • Covers medical, sickness and maternity benefits
  • Deposit by the 15th of next month

Professional Tax (PT)

  • State-level tax on salary, slab based (max ₹2,500 a year)
  • Rules, slabs and due dates differ by state
  • Deducted from salary and paid by employer

Labour Welfare Fund

  • Small state-wise contribution by employer and employee
  • Check your state’s rate and due date

Good habits for payroll compliance

  • Keep a monthly checklist and tick everything off
  • Never miss a due date: interest and penalties add up fast
  • Reconcile payroll register with challans and returns
  • Keep records (registers, challans, declarations) safe and confidential
  • Track law and rate changes after every Budget and notification
Study notes for fundamentals only, not tax or legal advice. Rates, limits and form names change; verify on official portals (incometax.gov.in, epfindia.gov.in, esic.gov.in) before use.
